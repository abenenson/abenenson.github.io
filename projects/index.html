Machine-Checked AI Governance

legitimacy
Rust · Lean 4 · v1.1.1 public
As AI agents allocate resources, delegate work, and act together, alignment becomes a question about the organization of power. Legitimacy studies the rules that could make this autonomy trustworthy: which guarantees can coexist, how decisions can be checked and corrected, and what happens when individually approved actions combine into an unsafe result. Lean proofs, Rust diagnostics, and a runnable composition experiment make those questions inspectable. Explore the research or open the browser example.

Quantitative Systems

Prism
Python · Svelte · CPCV/DSR-gated
A general-purpose portfolio-intelligence engine. Bayesian view translation (entropy pooling over Black-Litterman), instrumented-PCA factor decomposition, RMT-cleaned covariance (Bouchaud RIE, Ledoit-Wolf NLS), sparse-cardinality mean-CVaR optimization, graphical-matching pair selection, and an immutable decision ledger. Every result must survive a deflation-aware validation battery — combinatorially-purged cross-validation, deflated Sharpe, probability of backtest overfitting — before it is allowed to claim anything. In production in a live long/short equity consulting engagement, driving book analytics, hedge construction, and optimization counterfactuals.
Snowball
Rust
A recursively self-learning alpha compounding engine for prediction markets. Hypotheses enter as replay-scored candidates, survive shadow and paper validation, earn size through bounded live review, and get clawed back when evidence degrades. Every fill updates a market factor graph; the graph reshapes candidate selection; better candidates generate more evidence.

Workgraph
Rust · Python · TypeScript
A permissioned research-intelligence platform for professional services firms — domain ontologies spanning legal matters and investment research (firms, strategies, factors, themes). Email/Drive change-data-capture into a temporal-hypergraph knowledge graph with tri-temporal indexing; specialized agentic workflows for retrieval, drafting, compliance, and escalation; scoped ACLs with information-barrier enforcement and a full audit plane.
Digital Twin
Python · Rust · JavaScript
A personal digital twin built to reconstruct and extend causal reasoning, belief structure, and intellectual trajectory. Maintains a 24,000-episode store, 12,000-entry profile database, and a queryable knowledge graph. Context engineering formalized as a DT-MDP with a contrastive IRL retrieval policy. Drives daily development across the lab's other systems — including the legitimacy research program below.
Eudaimon
Rust
A recursively self-building factory for autonomous agent swarms. Every autonomy expansion is backed by a promotion certificate: evidence trail, admissibility check, rollback witness. The governance layer is the growth mechanism — a system that can only expand by proving it should.

Formal Methods

Stigmergic Field Theory
Lean 4
A formal theory of the relationship between information and energy, and how organized structure emerges from their interaction. Derives a variational principle and a three-component tensor decomposition (Integration, Complexity, Uncertainty) from a single information-geometric constraint. Key theorems sorry-free.

Open-source Lean 4 libraries built on Mathlib. The repositories describe the theorem scope and pin the Lean and Mathlib versions.

Probability and control

  • finite-markov-processes. Continuous-time jump processes, path large deviations, and particle confidence bounds.
  • finite-markov-kernels. Finite kernels, stationary laws, mixing, disintegration, and perturbation bounds.
  • finite-causal-control. Entropy-regularized control with Bellman regret and uniqueness on the selected support.
  • measure-discrimination. Lossless measurable encodings, density transport, and product likelihoods.

Information theory and geometry

  • finite-relative-entropy. Gibbs identities, Leinster’s KL characterization, strong data processing, and censoring loss.
  • channel-capacity. Uniqueness of capacity-achieving priors for Markov kernels. Overview.
  • fisher-information. KL curvature and expected squared score in finite and integral models.
  • cencov-petz. Uniqueness of the Fisher metric under finite Markov morphisms, up to scale.

Analysis, optimization, and geometry

  • levi-civita-smoothness. Global smoothness of Mathlib’s Levi-Civita connection for smooth Riemannian metrics.
  • rellich-kondrachov. Compact Sobolev embedding on compact Riemannian manifolds.
  • compact-spectral. The spectral theorem for compact self-adjoint operators.
  • berge-maximum. Berge’s maximum theorem for correspondences in a compact decision space.

Logic

  • godel-loeb. Löb’s theorem in a modal provability-logic framework.
